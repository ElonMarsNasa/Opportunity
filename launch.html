Launch day

Hi, I'm Opportunity, a robot geologist. I'm tucked inside the nose of a Delta II Heavy rocket at Cape Canaveral, Florida. Tonight, July 7, 2003, we leave Earth for Mars. It's a journey of more than six months, and I can't wait to see it. Will you count down with me?

Look for the glowing dot on the rocket. That's me inside the nose cone.

Science: why a Delta II Heavy?

Opportunity's launch window needed more energy than her twin Spirit's, so she flew on the more powerful Heavy version of the Delta II, which has larger solid boosters strapped to its sides.
