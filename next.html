Touchdown in Eagle Crater

I made it! After bouncing across Meridiani Planum in my airbags, I'm resting on my landing platform, ready for my first drive.

What I carry
Cameras, a rock grinder, a microscope and chemistry tools
What powers me
Solar panels, rechargeable batteries and small heaters for freezing nights
How I move
Six wheels, creeping along at a few centimeters per second
