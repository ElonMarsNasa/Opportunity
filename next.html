Touchdown in Eagle Crater

I did it! After bouncing across the surface in my airbags, I came to rest in a small crater on a flat plain called Meridiani Planum, in January 2004. Now I'm sitting on my landing platform and getting ready for my very first drive.

Where I am
Eagle Crater, Meridiani Planum, Mars
What I carry
Cameras for eyes, a rock grinder, a microscope camera, and tools that read what rocks are made of
What powers me
Solar panels charge my rechargeable batteries, and small heaters keep my electronics warm through freezing nights
How I move
Six wheels, each with its own motor. I creep along at only a few centimeters per second
